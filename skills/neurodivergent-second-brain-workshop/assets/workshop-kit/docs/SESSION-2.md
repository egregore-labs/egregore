# Session 2 — keep what survived real use

Duration: 90 minutes, several days after session 1. Outcome: simplify from lived evidence, validate the portable profile, export, and leave with a private emissary link.

## Re-entry — 0–15

- Show the written agenda and visible timer.
- Re-state pass/pause/leave/re-enter and privacy choices.
- Begin at [[Re-entry]], not with a recap round.

## What happened in real use — 15–35

- Participants may respond privately, in writing, aloud, or not at all.
- Ask: what did you return to, what caused friction, and what did you ignore?
- No forced disclosure of note content. Discuss interaction and structure only.

## Simplify — 35–55

- Remove or rename one piece that created friction.
- Keep the four-door core unless the participant explicitly chooses otherwise.
- Validate that capture and re-entry still work manually.
- Update `System/Configuration Profile.md` and `.second-brain/configuration-profile.json` together.

## Portability rehearsal — 55–70

- Ask the agent to explain how a future agent should configure Egregore and Obsidian from the profile.
- Check that no diagnosis, note content, provider key, or workshop token appears in the profile.
- Participant approves the profile as a representation of preferences, not identity.

## Export, emissary, deletion — 70–90

- Run the verified export with the participant present.
- Open or copy the vault export before deletion.
- Preview the private emissary’s legible layer and mandate.
- Publish only after **Go** and hand back the page link.
- Revoke the token, delete the workspace, show the deletion receipt, and reset loaners.
