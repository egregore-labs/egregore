# Consent and access script

Read or provide this in writing at the start of both sessions. Adapt the language with participants before production.

You control your participation. You may answer aloud, in writing, privately, or not at all. You may pass, pause, move, leave, and return without explanation. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis, medical history, or the contents of your notes.

Your Obsidian vault is private by default. The facilitator-funded AI gateway can send text you choose to a configured inference provider. The gateway records a pseudonymous participant ID, provider, status, timing, and token counts; it does not intentionally log prompts or outputs. Do not put anything into an AI prompt that you do not want processed by the configured provider.

Your workshop agent uses a temporary quota-bound token. It does not receive the facilitator’s provider keys. At exit, we will export and verify your vault with you, optionally create a private configuration emissary that excludes note contents, revoke the token, and delete the workshop workspace. You choose where your export goes and whether the emissary is published.

The manual Obsidian workflow remains available at every step. Using the agent is optional.
