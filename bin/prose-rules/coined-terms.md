A coined term is a new name for something the organization already has a word for, or a metaphor promoted to a label. "The harvest-fold," "a context-weave," "the fabric" name nothing a reader can look up, so every reader either guesses or asks, and two writers coining independently produce two names for one thing. Use the established term: handoff, decision, quest, surface, proposal, retrieval packet. If a thing has no name yet, describe it in plain words until a name is decided and recorded. Metaphor belongs in visuals and essays, never in labels, headings, field names, or command output.

## Fails

- the session's afterglow persists in the harvest-fold
- route it through the context-weave before it reaches the fabric

## Passes

- the handoff stays open until the receiver closes it
- route it through the retrieval packet before it reaches the checker
